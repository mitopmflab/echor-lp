This Policy explains how information is obtained, used, and stored in the app "echor" (the "App"). The App is operated by the sole proprietorship "dannpen" (the "Operator").

Versions covered

This Policy is written based on how information is handled in version 2.0.0 and later of the App. Versions earlier than 2.0.0 differ in the following respects:

  • There is no paid plan (echor+), and RevenueCat is not used. No purchase-related information is handled.
  • The widget, Sources, and multiple Gardens features are not available.
  • Firebase Analytics does not collect data on devices determined to be in a consent region (such as the EU/EEA) until you allow it; on other devices, it may be enabled if no explicit refusal has previously been confirmed.
  • Firebase Crashlytics is used regardless of region. There is no mechanism that keeps Firebase from starting based on the device region setting (see "4."), and none of the handling for devices determined to be in mainland China (Annex A).
  • The configuration file is retrieved (see "4.") regardless of region.

When you update the App to version 2.0.0 or later, the handling described in this Policy applies.

1. Information handled by the App

The App may handle the following information.

Information you enter or select

  • The text of your Words (memos), and Sources
  • The text of your Notes
  • The names and structure of your Gardens (units for grouping your Words)
  • Creation date and time, and update date and time
  • Data related to your Words and Notes
  • Files you select when backing up and restoring (the contents of these files are never sent to the Operator)

Information related to App settings

  • Notification on/off and notification time
  • Onboarding completion status
  • The content and settings shown in the widget (stored in a shared area on your device)
  • Other in-app settings

Information related to purchases of the paid plan (echor+) (version 2.0.0 and later)

  • Purchase and subscription status (such as the plan you subscribe to, the expiration date, whether auto-renewal is on, and use of a free trial), and information about App Store transactions
  • An anonymous identifier created for each device to manage purchases (if you delete and reinstall the App, a new one is created)
  • Technical information about the device and the App (such as the OS and its version, the device model, the App version, language settings, the App Store country, and the device identifier shared across apps from the same vendor (IDFV))
  • The IP address received in the course of communication

Information related to usage and crashes

  • Information obtained through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, as described in "4. External services"

The App does not obtain your name, email address, or phone number to manage purchases. Payment information, such as the card number used for payment, is handled by Apple and is not obtained by the App or the Operator. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not request permission to use it (App Tracking Transparency). The App also does not display advertising.

2. Purposes of use

The App handles information for the following purposes, depending on the type of information:

  • Saving and displaying your Words, Notes, Sources, and Gardens; providing notifications, the widget, and backup and restore; and maintaining in-app settings
  • Confirming purchases, restorations, and free trials of the paid plan, and managing subscription status (version 2.0.0 and later)
  • Improving the quality of the App, understanding usage, analyzing and fixing issues, and operating the service stably (information obtained through Firebase)
  • Responding to inquiries

The App does not use the content of the Words or Notes you enter for advertising, usage analysis, quality improvement, or issue analysis.

3. Data storage

  • The Words, Notes, Sources, Gardens, and settings information you enter are stored mainly on your own device. To display them in the widget, some of this information is also stored in a shared area on your device.
  • At present, the App does not send or store the text of your Words or Notes on servers managed by the Operator.
  • When you use the backup feature, data such as your Words, Notes, Sources, and Gardens is exported as a JSON file and saved to the save or sharing destination you choose. When you use the restore feature, the App reads the backup file you choose on your device. Any temporary copy created for processing is deleted after it has been read. The App does not send or store backup files or their contents on servers managed by the Operator.
  • If you choose iCloud Drive or another external service as the save or sharing destination, the handling of information by that service is governed by the terms and privacy policy of the relevant service provider.

4. External services

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve quality and analyze usage. In version 2.0.0 and later, the App does not start Firebase on devices determined to be in mainland China (Annex A). In addition, in version 2.0.0 and later, the App checks the device region setting at launch. On devices determined to be in a region that the App treats as a "consent region," such as the EU/EEA, the App normally does not start Firebase (Analytics or Crashlytics). Once a device has been so determined, the App normally continues not to start Firebase, even if the device region setting is changed later.

About Firebase Analytics

  • In version 2.0.0 and later, on devices determined to be in a consent region, the App normally neither collects data through Firebase Analytics nor shows the prompt to allow or decline. The same applies if you previously allowed it. The in-app Settings screen shows that usage analytics is not being performed on this device, and lets you withdraw a previous permission.
  • In version 2.0.0 and later, on devices where Firebase starts, Firebase Analytics does not collect data until you allow it. The App shows a prompt to allow or decline the first time, and starts collection only if you choose "Allow." You can use all features even if you do not allow it. Even after allowing it, you can withdraw at any time from "Usage analytics" in the in-app Settings screen, and you can also allow it again. If you chose to allow or decline in a previous version, that choice is carried over. Note that Firebase Crashlytics is used independently of whether you allow or decline Analytics (see below). To determine whether a device is in a consent region, the App's implementation uses the device region setting, not the App's language setting. This determination does not establish your legal place of residence.
  • Information that may be collected: approximate geographic location (at roughly the country/region level) derived by Google / device and app instance identifiers / actions within the App / use of screens and features / other usage data / OS information, device type, and App version / App usage metrics aggregated within predefined fixed ranges
  • In the Firebase (GA4) property, the retention period for user-level and event-level data is set to 14 months. For user-level data, the setting resets the retention period when new activity is recorded. Note that separate retention rules may apply to aggregated reports and the like on Google's side, and not all Analytics data is deleted after 14 months.

About Firebase Crashlytics

  • Crashlytics is used on devices where Firebase starts (in version 2.0.0 and later, excluding devices determined to be in mainland China and devices determined to be in a consent region; in versions earlier than 2.0.0, it is used regardless of region), independently of whether you allow or decline Analytics, to obtain diagnostic information about crashes and about quality and stability. The App does not set a custom user identifier in Crashlytics. Custom keys and logs sent to Crashlytics are limited to predetermined operational information, and the App does not intentionally send the text of your Words or Notes or any other free-form text. However, information such as crash occurrences, diagnostic information, and information about the device and the App may be processed.
  • Versions of the App earlier than 2.0.0 did not stop Firebase based on the device region setting. As a result, crash information created on the device by a previous version that has not yet been sent may remain.
    • On devices determined to be in a consent region, the App normally does not start Firebase, and therefore normally does not send this information. The App does not delete this information from the device, but it is deleted together with the data on the device when you delete the App.
    • On devices that were not determined to be in a consent region at the first launch of version 2.0.0 or later, this information may be sent when Firebase starts.
    • This explanation does not apply to information that a previous version has already sent.
  • According to Firebase's official documentation, crash stack traces and related identifiers are retained for 90 days after collection, after which deletion begins. In addition, the App currently has the Crash Insights feature intentionally enabled. Through this feature, crash information may be compared with crash information from other apps that use Firebase, for the purpose of analyzing crash trends in an anonymized and aggregated form.

Common to both

  • The App does not send information that directly identifies an individual, such as your name or email address, to Firebase.
  • The App does not send the text of your Words, the text of your Notes, the contents of backup files, file names, URIs, or save destination paths to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. In addition, the App does not set its own user ID as the Firebase Analytics user_id or as a Firebase Crashlytics custom user identifier.
  • Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat (managing purchase and subscription status; version 2.0.0 and later)

The App uses the services of RevenueCat, Inc. (United States; "RevenueCat") to confirm purchases, restorations, and free trials of the paid plan, and to manage subscription status. RevenueCat processes the following information on behalf of the Operator:

  • The anonymous identifier for each device, purchase and subscription status and transaction information, and technical information about the device and the App (including the IDFV)
  • The IP address received in the course of communication

The App does not send the text of your Words or Notes, your name, email address, or phone number to RevenueCat. The Operator does not set an App-specific user ID and instead uses the anonymous identifier created by RevenueCat.

The following points about RevenueCat are based on answers the Operator has received from RevenueCat:

  • Information sent to RevenueCat is stored in the United States.
  • IP addresses received by RevenueCat are not stored in customer profiles. For logs, metadata, and the like other than customer profiles, the retention period varies by type of information and processing system, and RevenueCat has not provided a fixed maximum retention period.
  • Where the device identifier (IDFV) is included in RevenueCat's operational request logs, it is retained in those logs for up to 60 days.
  • Once communication with RevenueCat has started, RevenueCat's mechanisms may continue communicating until the App is closed. It is not possible to stop communication partway through or to discard data that has not yet been sent.
  • Before using RevenueCat, the App checks information such as the App Store country/region and the device region setting. In some regions (Annex A: mainland China), the App does not use RevenueCat until you give consent.

For retention periods and the scope of deletion, see "9. Deleting data and your rights" and "10. Retention periods." RevenueCat's Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple (App Store)

Purchases, payments, cancellations, refunds, and purchase records for the paid plan are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple's handling of this information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/jp/legal/privacy/

Retrieval of the configuration file (the Operator's website)

At launch and at other times, the App may retrieve a configuration file used for purposes such as App update notices from the Operator's website (echor.dannpen.com). During this retrieval, the App does not send identifiers or user information. However, as part of operating the website, information that normally accompanies communication, such as the source IP address, may be recorded. In version 2.0.0 and later, this retrieval is not performed on devices determined to be in mainland China.

Website analytics

The Operator's website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page views and traffic trends. According to Cloudflare's official documentation, Cloudflare Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors' personal data and does not use client-side state, such as cookies or localStorage, to measure usage. Cloudflare's Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

Feedback form

When you open "Feedback & Requests" in the App, a Google Form opens in your browser. The content you enter in the form is sent to the Operator through Google's services. The content you enter is handled as described in "13. Information obtained through inquiries, registrations for updates, and similar means."

5. Notifications

The App uses local notifications.

  • Notifications may display part or all of the text of the Words (memos) you have saved.
  • These notifications may appear on your device's lock screen or in Notification Center.
  • You can turn off notifications yourself in your device settings or in the in-app settings.

6. AI and machine learning

  • The App does not currently use the content of the Words or Notes you enter for AI training or machine learning. In addition, at present, the App does not send the content of your Words or Notes to external AI services.
  • If AI-related features are added in the future, the Operator will revise this Policy and provide any necessary notice.

7. Provision to third parties and entrustment

Except as required by law, the Operator does not provide the content of the Words or Notes you enter to third parties. The Operator entrusts the processing of information, within the scope described, to the service providers listed in "4. External services" (such as Google, RevenueCat, and Cloudflare), or uses their services.

8. Security

The App strives to take appropriate security measures to prevent unauthorized access, information leakage, tampering, and the like. However, complete security is not guaranteed.

9. Deleting data and your rights

  • You can delete Words and Notes saved in the App yourself. Normally, when you delete the App from your device, the data stored on the device is also deleted. However, backup files exported from the App, and files saved to the Files app, iCloud Drive, or other save destinations, are not automatically deleted when you delete the App. Please delete any files you no longer need yourself.

Deleting the App does not automatically delete information that has already been sent to external services such as RevenueCat, or purchase records managed by Apple. In addition, deleting the App does not cancel your subscription.

Under applicable laws, you may be able to request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, deletion, and the like of your information. Such requests are accepted at the email address listed in "15. Operator and contact." When handling a request, the Operator may ask for information needed to verify your identity and to identify the information concerned.

11. For users in the EEA, France, and other regions

Users in regions including the EEA (European Economic Area) and France should note the following:

  • In version 2.0.0 and later, on devices determined to be in a consent region, the App normally does not start Firebase (Analytics or Crashlytics) (see "4."). If you allow Analytics on any other device, you can withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • Under applicable data protection laws, you may have rights regarding your information, such as the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability.
  • Users residing in France may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés).
  • Requests to exercise the above rights are accepted at the email address listed in "15. Operator and contact." General questions about this Policy can also be sent from "Feedback & Requests" in the App.
  • Service providers such as Google/Firebase and RevenueCat may process information outside Japan (including outside the EEA). The safeguards for the handling of information outside Japan vary depending on the service provider, the information processed, and the applicable contractual terms. For details, please see each service provider's privacy policy and related documents, or contact us as described in "15. Operator and contact." Regarding safeguards for handling outside Japan, RevenueCat publishes a Data Processing Addendum (including provisions on Standard Contractual Clauses) that is incorporated into its terms of service. Google has established the Data Processing and Security Terms for Firebase Crashlytics, and data processing terms for Google Analytics. RevenueCat's Data Processing Addendum (https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa) and Firebase's Data Processing and Security Terms (https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms) are published on each company's website.
  • Whether a device is in a consent region is determined from the device region setting, and this does not establish your place of residence. Even if you reside in the EEA, if your device region setting does not fall within a consent region and your device has never been determined to be in a consent region, the handling by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics described in "4." may take place. In version 2.0.0 and later, in that case as well, Firebase Analytics does not collect data until you allow it. Firebase Crashlytics is used independently of whether you allow or decline Analytics (see "4.").
  • In version 2.0.0 and later, usage analysis by Firebase Analytics is carried out on the legal basis of your explicit consent (choosing "Allow" in the prompt, or turning on "Usage analytics" in the Settings screen). You can withdraw your consent at any time, and no new data is collected after withdrawal.
  • The handling of crash and reliability information may be based on the legitimate interests of operating the App stably and maintaining its security; however, this does not mean that other legal requirements, such as consent, are uniformly unnecessary as a result.

In some regions, the handling of information is adjusted in light of applicable laws. For the EEA and France, see "11."; for mainland China, see "Annex A."

Annex A — For users in mainland China (version 2.0.0 and later)

On devices determined to be in mainland China, the App behaves as follows. The determination is based on information such as the App Store country/region or the device region setting.

  1. The App does not start Firebase (Analytics or Crashlytics).
  2. The App does not retrieve the configuration file from the Operator's website.
  3. The App uses RevenueCat to confirm purchases, restorations, and free trials of the paid plan only if you give consent on screen. Before you give consent, if you do not give consent, and at any new launch after you have withdrawn consent, the App does not initialize RevenueCat.
  4. Even without consent, purchases of the paid plan that can be confirmed from this device's App Store purchase records are reflected in the App. Even if you do not give consent, you can use all free features.
  5. You can withdraw your consent at any time from the App's Settings screen. After withdrawal, the App does not make new requests to RevenueCat. However, while the App is running, RevenueCat's mechanisms may continue communicating. If you give consent again after withdrawing it, unsent data remaining on the device may be sent.
  6. The information sent, where it is stored, and how long it is retained are as described in "4." and "10."
  7. Inquiries about personal information and requests for disclosure, deletion, and the like are accepted through the contact listed in "15. Operator and contact."

The above is a description based on the App's implementation. It does not guarantee that no communication takes place at all.
